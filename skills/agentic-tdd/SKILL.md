# Agentic TDD

## Purpose

Perform a full TDD workflow utilising our existing tdd skillset.

## Local Guidance

Always check for coding standards in `AGENTS.MD`, `CLAUDE.MD`, existing skills, rules, etc... when available, follow their guidance.

## Workflow

Execute the following pre-existing skills in order:
1. `test-review`
2. `scaffold-test`
3. `add-test-cases`
